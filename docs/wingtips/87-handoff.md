# Hermes Wingtips #87: /handoff, move a live terminal chat to your phone's messaging app with its full history

posted 2026-09-30. original: [https://x.com/witcheer/status/2105222388530946090](https://x.com/witcheer/status/2105222388530946090)

you are mid-conversation with your Hermes Agent in the terminal and you have to leave your desk.

type this in the chat:

`/handoff telegram`

the session moves to your Telegram home channel with its full history, and the agent replies there with a short summary of where you left off. you carry on from your phone.

useful when you head out mid-project, or when a chat you started at your desk needs finishing on the train!

docs: [https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/user-guide/sessions#cross-platform-handoff](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/user-guide/sessions#cross-platform-handoff)
