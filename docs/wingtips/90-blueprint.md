# Hermes Wingtips #90: /blueprint, a ready-made automation scheduled as a cron job, no cron syntax

posted 2026-10-03. original: [https://x.com/witcheer/status/2106275419557126419](https://x.com/witcheer/status/2106275419557126419)

it sets up a ready-made automation for your Hermes Agent. Hermes asks you what it needs, one question at a time, then schedules it as a cron job. you do not write any cron syntax.

want a short lesson on a topic you pick, every weekday morning?

type this in the chat:

`/blueprint learn-daily`

other blueprints cover a reminder in your own words, or a weekly meal plan with the grocery list!

`/blueprint` on its own lists every blueprint your Hermes Agent has.
